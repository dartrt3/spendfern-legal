Support

Email spendfern@gmail.com. Include your phone model and a short description, and send it from your Spendfern account email if your question is about your account. Never send your password.

Sign-in and account

  • Forgot password: on the sign-in screen, enter your email and tap Forgot password?. Check your spam or promotions folder for the email.
  • Verification email: open Plan & account to resend it. A verified email is needed to unlock scans with rewarded ads.
  • "Couldn't connect": check your internet connection and try again.

Expenses, income and history

Use Add for expenses and income. History shows each month, and Categories shows where your money went. Open a transaction to see its details or delete it.

Receipt scanning

  • Use a flat, well-lit photo with the whole receipt visible.
  • Check every suggested value before saving. AI suggestions can be wrong.
  • Scanning works only in the app installed from Google Play, on a device that passes Google's integrity checks.
  • Scanning may sometimes be temporarily unavailable. You can always add expenses manually.

Rewarded ads (Free plan)

  • On the Free plan, watching a rewarded ad to the end unlocks one AI scan attempt.
  • The credit is used when the attempt starts, even if the receipt cannot be read.
  • The number of rewarded credits is limited and set by our server.
  • If no ad is available, try again later or add the expense manually.
  • Please don't click ads just to support us.
  • To change ad consent, use Ad privacy choices in Plan & account when it is shown.

Spendfern Pro

Pro includes up to 100 successful AI receipt scans per UTC month without ads. It is billed monthly or yearly by Google Play and renews until you cancel. To cancel, change or ask about a refund, use Google Play subscriptions. Refunds follow Google Play's policies.

Restore purchases

Sign in to the same Spendfern account, open Plan & account and tap Restore purchases. Make sure Google Play is signed in with the Google account that bought the subscription.

Data and account deletion

See Delete your account and our privacy policy. To request a copy of your data, email us from your account address.
